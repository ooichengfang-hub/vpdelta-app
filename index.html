Volume + Delta profile

VOLUME = how much traded at each price level (gray bars). The brightest bar is the POC (point of control). Dotted lines mark the 70% value area (VAH/VAL).

DELTA = who was aggressive at each level: buy volume − sell volume. Green → right = BUYERS, purple ← left = SELLERS.

Approximation: delta is estimated from each candle's taker-buy volume (Binance kline field), not from tick-level footprint data. Each candle's volume and delta are spread across the price bins between its low and high: 60% evenly over the full wick range and 40% over the body. On 5m/15m/1h charts the profile is built from lower-timeframe candles (1m/5m/15m) where available, which is more precise.

LIQ = forced liquidations on Binance USDT-M futures, in USDT notional (price × qty) per price level. Red ← left = LONGS liquidated (forced sells), cyan → right = SHORTS liquidated (forced buys). Dots on the candles mark each liquidation, sized by notional (toggle with the LIQ button).

Liquidations are a sample, not complete history. Binance offers no public liquidation history, and its live feed sends at most one liquidation per symbol per second (the largest). So this panel only fills while the page is open, starting from the "since" time shown above the LIQ panel. Collected events are kept on this device (up to 5,000 per symbol, 7 days) for symbols you have viewed.

The countdown under the last-price tag is the time left until the current candle closes, synced to Binance server time.

Profiles cover only the candles currently on screen and update when you zoom or pan.

ABS (heuristic, not a signal): flags a price bin that is a high-volume node (top 20%) with strongly negative delta (bottom 15%) where a candle's low printed and the next 5 candles made no lower low — i.e. selling that may have been absorbed.

WICK bubbles = volume traded at the tip of a candle: the top 10% of its high–low range (green, at the high) and the bottom 10% (red, at the low). Colour shows position, not delta; tap the candle to see that tip's buy/sell delta. Numbers are in the base coin (e.g. BTC for BTCUSDT): 139 = 139 BTC. A thick, bright ring = heavy: more than 2× the average tip volume on screen, a hint of absorption. Bubbles show only on swing highs/lows (±2 candles) and heavy tips to stay readable.

How it is computed: exact from Binance public aggTrades (each trade's price, size and aggressor side), live from the aggTrade stream plus a REST back-fill of the most recent candles (about 30 on 1m, the latest few on higher timeframes). Older 5m/15m/1h candles use an estimate from 1m candles (dashed ring). Older 1m candles without trade data get no bubble.

BOOK = current order book on the price axis: green bids, red asks, summed into the chart's price bins (Binance REST depth, 1000 levels every 5 s, plus the live top-20 stream). Only shows resting limit orders right now, which can be pulled at any time.

ZONE: tap ZONE, then drag on the chart to draw a box. Tap a zone to select it and delete it. Zones are saved per symbol on this device.

PAPER trading — simulated only. LONG/SHORT opens a practice position at the live price (size in the coin or USDT). Default stop-loss 0.25% and take-profit 0.5% away; drag the SL/TP lines to move them. They close the position automatically when the live price touches them (after reopening the app, missed hits are checked from the chart's candles; if both were touched in one candle, the stop is assumed first). PnL is in USDT and includes a 0.05% taker fee on entry and exit. One position per symbol. History and total are kept on this device. No real orders are ever sent.

Controls: drag to pan, pinch / scroll to zoom, tap (or hover) for candle and price-level details.

Data: Binance public market data (USDT-M futures; spot only as fallback). No account, no keys. Times in Asia/Kuala_Lumpur (UTC+8). Not financial advice.

—Δ ≈ taker-buy approx.
